## Checks
- PASS Same-day is only promised before the cutoff, or on the afternoon wave (0 same-day promises made after 08:00 outside the wave)
- PASS No warehouse picks more afternoon-wave orders than its slots (0 warehouse-days over 15)
- PASS Stock never goes negative (0 negative stock rows)
- PASS An order with no stock is never counted delivered (0 delivered without stock)
- PASS Every destination maps to its region (as the carrier plan reads it) (0 addresses map to the wrong region)
- PASS A lower-scoring carrier is only booked when every better one is full (0 orders skipped a carrier with room)
- PASS No carrier is booked past its daily capacity in a region (0 carrier-days over capacity)
- PASS Scoring matches the agent's rules (F, Midwest, same-day) (F scores 80.21)
- PASS 'Before', shipped from the nearest warehouse, matches history (within 4 pts) (simulated 73.0% vs history 74.4% on 1634 orders)
- PASS 'Before' on time matches history (within 3 pts) (simulated 70.0% vs history 71.0%)
- PASS Same-day before 08:00 from the nearest warehouse is the best case (history 86.1%)

## Assumptions
- Clients answer an address question 80% of the time, within 0.5–6 hours.
- 2–40 cartons a line, 1–3 lines an order; no restocking during the week.
- Stock-outs at the nearest warehouse happen as often as in history; an approved AI transfer (FR-07) prevents 50% of them. The data can't give this share (A8); at 0% the transfers add nothing.
- Afternoon wave (proposed, not built): same-day orders complete between the cutoff and 13:00 are picked at 14:00, up to 15 a warehouse a day (pick capacity isn't recorded, A7). They are judged like same-day orders picked that morning, scaled by how those did when collected at 15:00 or later: 85.4% on time (factor 0.991); 26.8% of collections run that late.
- A same-day order moved to the next day is as likely on time as the dataset's next-day orders.
- A carrier takes at most its historical daily volume in a region plus 30%; beyond that Halcyon books the next-best carrier.
- A carrier changes the on-time chance by its late rate into the region against the historical mix there; a late order costs $34 to redeliver.
- Destination cities are examples in the order's real region; the region is the dataset's.
